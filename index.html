Lumen

A beautifully simple IPTV player for your TV.

Lumen is designed to feel like it came with your TV: calm, fast and polished, with nothing in the way of what you're watching. Live TV with a full channel guide, your favourite channels front and centre, and films and series with Continue watching, all in one clean place.

Lumen is only a player. It comes with no channels or playlists: you add your own M3U link or Xtream login.

Works on

Android TV and Google TV smart TVs, Chromecast with Google TV, Nvidia Shield, Android TV boxes, and Amazon Fire TV sticks running Fire OS 8 (such as the Fire TV Stick 4K and 4K Max). Needs Android 10 or newer.

Install

  1. Install the free Downloader app (by AFTVnews) from your TV's app store.
  2. Allow Downloader to install apps. Most TVs ask the first time. If yours doesn't:
    • Android TV / Google TV: Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → Downloader
    • Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps → Downloader
  3. Open Downloader and enter:
    lumen-tv.github.io/get
  4. Choose Install, then open Lumen from your apps.

Lumen keeps itself up to date: when a new version is out, it lets you know on its Home screen.
