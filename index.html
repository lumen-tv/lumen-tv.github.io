Get Lumen on your TV

It takes about a minute. Works on Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV running Android 10 or newer.

  1. Install the free Downloader app from your TV's app store.
  2. Let Downloader install apps. Most TVs ask the first time; if not, see the tip below.
  3. Open Downloader and enter:
    lumen-tv.github.io/get
  4. Choose Install, then open Lumen from your apps. That's it.

Tip: on Android TV and Google TV it's Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources. On Fire TV it's Settings → My Fire TV → Developer options → Install unknown apps.

Questions

Everything you might want to know.

Does Lumen come with channels?

No. Lumen is only a player: it comes with no channels, playlists or films. You add your own M3U playlist link or Xtream login from a TV service you already use.

Which TVs does it work on?

Android TV and Google TV televisions, streaming sticks and boxes, and Fire TV devices on Fire OS 8 or newer. Lumen needs Android 10 or newer.

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no account to make.

How do updates work?

When a new version is out, Lumen tells you on its Home screen. Choose Update, confirm, and the new version is ready in moments.

Is there a TV guide?

Yes. Lumen loads your service's guide automatically, or you can add a separate guide link in Settings. It refreshes itself in the background.

What does Lumen know about me?

Nothing. Your playlists, logins and favourites stay on your TV. Lumen has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
