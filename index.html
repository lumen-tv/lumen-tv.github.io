Lumen

A minimal, Apple TV–style IPTV player for Google TV.

Live TV with a channel guide, favourites on the home screen, and films and series from your provider. Lumen is only a player: it comes with no channels or playlists. You add your own M3U link or Xtream login.

Install on Google TV

  1. Install Downloader (by AFTVnews) from the Play Store on your TV.
  2. Allow it to install apps: Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions → Unknown sources → Downloader.
  3. Open Downloader and enter:
    lumen-tv.github.io/get
  4. Choose Install.

Lumen updates itself: when a new version is out, its Home screen offers it.
