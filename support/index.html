Support

Help for Lumen on Google TV, iPhone and iPad.

Getting started

Lumen plays the TV subscription you already have. Open Lumen, choose Add playlist, and enter the Xtream login (server, username, password) or the M3U link your provider gave you. Your channels, guide, films and series then load.

Try it without a subscription

https://lumen-tv.github.io/review/playlist.m3u

Common questions

  • Does Lumen come with channels? No. Lumen is only a player; it has no content of its own.
  • A film won't play. Close it and open it again. Some providers allow only one connection at a time, so stop playback on your other devices first.
  • No TV guide? The guide comes from your provider. In Settings, open your playlist and check its guide link.

Contact

Report a problem or ask a question by opening an issue on GitHub. Please never post your playlist link or login.
